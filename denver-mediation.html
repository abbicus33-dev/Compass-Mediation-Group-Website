Our Denver Mediation Services

Divorce Mediation in Denver

Divorce is one of life’s most stressful transitions — but it doesn’t have to be a battle. Our divorce mediation process helps couples in the Denver area divide assets, establish support arrangements, and create parenting plans without the adversarial nature of courtroom proceedings.

Colorado is an equitable-distribution state, which means marital property is divided fairly — though not always equally — based on the circumstances of each case. Colorado courts also strongly favor mediation, and many divorcing couples are required to attempt it. Understanding how the court approaches property, debts, retirement accounts, and maintenance requires careful analysis, and as an IRS Enrolled Agent (a credential Alisa earned in 2022, also applied through Compass Tax Center, our sister company specializing in tax services), she brings tax expertise most mediators simply cannot offer.

Family Mediation

Family disputes extend beyond divorce. Whether you need help with parenting-plan modifications, elder-care decisions, or conflicts between family members, our family mediation services provide a neutral, structured environment where everyone’s voice is heard.

Colorado uses the terms “parental responsibilities” and “parenting time” rather than custody. Drawing on her 16 years of family law and litigation experience, Alisa helps parents develop comprehensive plans that address decision-making, parenting-time schedules, dispute-resolution methods, and relocation provisions — all centered on the best interests of your children.

Business and Estate Mediation

Denver’s fast-growing economy — from energy and aerospace to a booming tech and small-business scene — generates its share of partnership, contract, and succession disputes that benefit from mediation over litigation. Our business and estate mediation services help stakeholders find workable solutions that preserve both relationships and assets. Alisa’s firsthand business-ownership experience — including founding and running Synergy Relations since 2003 — gives her practical insight into the realities business owners face.

Understanding Denver Court Procedures

If you’re considering divorce in the Denver area, your case will be filed in Denver District Court (or the district court for your county). Colorado law requires a 91-day waiting period before a divorce can be finalized, and courts often order mediation before a contested hearing.

Compass Mediation Group helps you understand what the court requires so your mediated agreements meet all legal standards. Alisa’s 16 years of prior legal practice and courtroom experience mean she understands the procedural landscape from the inside. Although mediators do not provide legal advice to either party, our process is designed to ensure your agreements are thorough, informed, and ready for court approval.

Why Virtual Mediation Is Ideal for Denver

I-25 congestion, sprawling suburbs, and Front Range weather can make in-person meetings a hassle. Virtual mediation eliminates that friction entirely, keeping the process convenient no matter where you live.

Our clients tell us that participating from home actually improves the process. You’re in a familiar environment where you feel safe and grounded, there’s no anxiety about a shared waiting room, and parents don’t need to arrange extra childcare for travel time.

Every virtual session is conducted on a secure, confidential platform with virtual breakout rooms, so each party can have private, confidential conversations with the mediator during the session.
