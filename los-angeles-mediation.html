Our Los Angeles Mediation Services

Divorce Mediation in Los Angeles

Divorce is one of life’s most stressful transitions — but it doesn’t have to be a battle. Our divorce mediation process helps couples in the LA area divide assets, establish support arrangements, and create parenting plans without the adversarial nature of courtroom proceedings.

California is a community property state, which means assets and debts acquired during the marriage are generally divided equally. Navigating community property — especially with real estate, business interests, and retirement accounts — requires careful analysis, and as an IRS Enrolled Agent (a credential Alisa earned in 2022, also applied through Compass Tax Center, our sister company specializing in tax services), she brings tax expertise most mediators simply cannot offer. Your settlement considers not just what’s fair today, but the long-term financial implications for both parties.

Family Mediation

Family disputes extend beyond divorce. Whether you need help with custody and parenting-plan modifications, elder-care decisions, or conflicts between family members, our family mediation services provide a neutral, structured environment where everyone’s voice is heard.

California courts prioritize the best interests of the child in every custody decision. Drawing on her 16 years of family law and litigation experience, Alisa helps parents develop comprehensive plans that address custody and visitation schedules, decision-making authority, dispute-resolution methods, and relocation provisions.

Business and Estate Mediation

Los Angeles is one of the world’s great business centers — from entertainment and tech to a vast small-business and creative community — and partnership, contract, and succession disputes are an inevitable part of that landscape. Our business and estate mediation services help stakeholders find workable solutions that preserve both relationships and assets. Alisa’s firsthand business-ownership experience — including founding and running Synergy Relations since 2003 — gives her practical insight into the realities business owners face during disputes.

Understanding Los Angeles County Court Procedures

If you’re considering divorce in the LA area, your case will be filed in Los Angeles County Superior Court — the largest trial court system in the nation. While California courts encourage mediation, navigating the procedural requirements, from the six-month waiting period to detailed financial disclosure obligations, can feel overwhelming.

Compass Mediation Group helps you understand what the court requires so your mediated agreements meet all legal standards. Alisa’s 16 years of prior legal practice and courtroom experience mean she understands the procedural landscape from the inside. Although mediators do not provide legal advice to either party, our process is designed to ensure your agreements are thorough, informed, and ready for court approval.

Why Virtual Mediation Is Ideal for Los Angeles

If you live in LA, you know the traffic reality: a cross-town meeting can eat half a day. Virtual mediation eliminates that friction entirely, so you never lose hours on the freeway for an appointment.

Our clients tell us that participating from home actually improves the process. You’re in a familiar environment where you feel safe and grounded, there’s no anxiety about a shared waiting room, and parents don’t need to arrange extra childcare for travel time.

Every virtual session is conducted on a secure, confidential platform with virtual breakout rooms, so each party can have private, confidential conversations with the mediator during the session.
