Preparation Checklists

Financial Documents to Gather:

  • Recent tax returns (2–3 years)
  • Pay stubs and income documentation
  • Bank account statements (checking, savings, investment)
  • Retirement account statements (401k, IRA, pension)
  • Mortgage documents and property valuations
  • Credit card and loan statements
  • Insurance policies
  • Business financial statements (if applicable)

Personal Documents to Organize:

  • Marriage certificate
  • Prenuptial or postnuptial agreements
  • Existing court orders
  • Vehicle titles and registrations
  • Estate planning documents (wills, trusts)

Pro tip: Alisa's certification as an IRS Enrolled Agent means she can help you understand the tax implications of asset division — an area where many mediators lack expertise. Be sure to ask about tax consequences during your sessions.

Business Mediation Resources

Business disputes can be costly and disruptive. Mediation offers a faster, more confidential, and more cost-effective path to resolution.

Our Guides

Business Mediation ServicesHow Compass Mediation Group helps businesses resolve conflicts and move forward.Is Mediation Legally Binding?Understanding the enforceability of business mediation agreements.

Common Business Mediation Scenarios

  • Partnership disputes and dissolutions
  • Shareholder and ownership conflicts
  • Contract disagreements
  • Employer-employee disputes
  • Vendor and supplier conflicts
  • Franchise disputes
  • Non-compete and intellectual property disagreements
  • Business succession and transition planning

Preparation Tips for Business Mediation

  • Review all relevant contracts and agreements before your session
  • Gather financial records related to the dispute
  • Document the timeline of events and key communications
  • Consider what a realistic resolution looks like for your business
  • Think about the ongoing relationship — will you continue working together?

External Resources

  • SCORE — Free business mentoring and resources from experienced professionals.
  • Small Business Administration (SBA) — Government resources for small businesses, including guidance on business disputes and legal matters.
  • U.S. Chamber of Commerce — Conflict Resolution — Business resources and advocacy.

Estate Mediation Resources

Disagreements over an estate can fracture families and drag on for years in probate court. Mediation provides a dignified, family-centered way to resolve estate disputes.

Our Guides

Estate Mediation ServicesHow Compass Mediation Group helps families resolve estate and inheritance disputes.Is Mediation Legally Binding?How estate mediation agreements interact with probate court requirements.

Common Estate Mediation Issues

  • Interpretation of wills and trust documents
  • Distribution of personal property and sentimental items
  • Real estate decisions (selling, keeping, or dividing property)
  • Executor or trustee performance disputes
  • Disagreements among beneficiaries
  • Claims of undue influence or lack of capacity
  • Blended family inheritance conflicts
  • Charitable bequest disputes

What to Bring to Estate Mediation

  • The will, trust, or estate planning documents
  • Inventory of estate assets
  • Property appraisals or valuations
  • Correspondence among the parties
  • Any relevant court filings
  • A list of concerns and priorities

External Resources

  • American Bar Association — Estate Planning Resources — Educational resources on estate and trust law.
  • National Academy of Elder Law Attorneys (NAELA) — Find elder law attorneys and estate planning resources.
  • Nolo.com — Estate & Probate — Accessible legal guides on estate planning, probate, and inheritance.
