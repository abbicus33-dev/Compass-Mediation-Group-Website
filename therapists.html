Compass Mediation Group works in concert with family therapy

At Compass Mediation Group, our story is rooted in a simple belief: families deserve healthier ways to resolve conflict. That belief moved us from litigation in the 1990s to a full commitment to mediation today.

Compass Mediation Group is dedicated to creating healthier ways for families to resolve their conflicts. Our team collaborates with family therapists to provide valuable resources, including family mediation tips, tailored to improve the mediation process. Whether you’re a therapist looking to support your clients or a family in need of guidance, our services are designed to empower and promote healing.

Drawing on decades of experience, we’ve created a resource designed specifically for family therapists. Download our free guide today to explore practical family mediation tips and start making a positive difference.
