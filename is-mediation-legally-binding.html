Is Mediation Legally Binding?

One of the most important questions people ask before choosing mediation: will the agreement actually hold up? Here's a clear explanation of how mediation agreements become legally binding and what protects you after you sign.

If you're considering mediation for a divorce, family dispute, business disagreement, or estate matter, you want to know that the time and effort you invest will result in something real — an agreement with legal weight that both parties must honor.

The short answer: yes, mediation agreements can absolutely be legally binding. But understanding when and how they become enforceable is important. Let's walk through the details.
