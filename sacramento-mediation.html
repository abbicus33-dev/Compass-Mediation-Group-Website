Our Sacramento Mediation Services

Divorce Mediation in Sacramento

Divorce is one of life’s most stressful transitions — but it doesn’t have to be a battle. Our divorce mediation process helps couples in the Sacramento area divide assets, establish support arrangements, and create parenting plans without the adversarial nature of courtroom proceedings.

California is a community property state, which means assets and debts acquired during the marriage are generally divided equally. Navigating community property — especially with real estate, retirement accounts, and public-sector pensions common in the capital region — requires careful analysis, and as an IRS Enrolled Agent (a credential Alisa earned in 2022, also applied through Compass Tax Center, our sister company specializing in tax services), she brings tax expertise most mediators simply cannot offer. Your settlement considers not just what’s fair today, but the long-term financial implications for both parties.

Family Mediation

Drawing on her 16 years of family law and litigation experience, Alisa helps parents develop comprehensive plans centered on the best interests of your children.

Business and Estate Mediation

Our business and estate mediation services help stakeholders find workable solutions that preserve both relationships and assets. Alisa’s firsthand business-ownership experience — including founding and running Synergy Relations since 2003 — gives her practical insight into the realities business owners face.

Understanding Sacramento County Court Procedures

If you’re considering divorce in the Sacramento area, your case will be filed in Sacramento County Superior Court. While California courts encourage mediation, navigating the procedural requirements, from the six-month waiting period to detailed financial disclosure obligations, can feel overwhelming.

Compass Mediation Group helps you understand what the court requires so your mediated agreements meet all legal standards. Alisa’s 16 years of prior legal practice and courtroom experience mean she understands the procedural landscape from the inside. Although mediators do not provide legal advice to either party, our process is designed to ensure your agreements are thorough, informed, and ready for court approval.

Why Virtual Mediation Is Ideal for Sacramento

From the suburbs of Placer and El Dorado counties into the city, and with summer heat that rivals anywhere in the state, driving across the region for an appointment that could happen online is simply unnecessary. Virtual mediation eliminates that friction entirely.

Our clients tell us that participating from home actually improves the process. You’re in a familiar environment where you feel safe and grounded, there’s no anxiety about a shared waiting room, and parents don’t need to arrange extra childcare for travel time.

Every virtual session is conducted on a secure, confidential platform with virtual breakout rooms, so each party can have private, confidential conversations with the mediator during the session.
