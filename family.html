Family Mediation

Family relationships are among the most important — and sometimes the most challenging — parts of our lives. When disagreements arise, unresolved conflict can fracture bonds that took decades to build. Family mediation offers a constructive, compassionate path forward — one that prioritizes understanding over winning.

Schedule a Free Consultation

At Compass Mediation Group, we specialize in helping families navigate conflict, rebuild communication, and reach agreements that everyone can live with. Our lead mediator, Alisa Kharis, brings 16 years of courtroom experience as a former practicing attorney in Washington state courts, Master Mediator certification from the International Mediation Association (IMA), and 25+ years of mediation experience — along with a genuine commitment to helping families find their way through difficult moments.

What Is Family Mediation?

Family mediation is a structured, confidential process where a neutral mediator helps family members work through disputes and reach mutually acceptable solutions. Unlike courtroom proceedings, family mediation creates a safe space for open dialogue — guided by a professional who ensures every voice is heard.

Family mediation can address virtually any conflict that arises between relatives, whether it involves parenting responsibilities, financial disagreements, caregiving decisions, or longstanding interpersonal tensions.

Types of Family Disputes We Mediate

Separation and Divorce-Related Family Issues

Divorce doesn't just affect spouses — it affects parents, children, in-laws, and extended family. We help families navigate the broader relational impact of divorce, including grandparent access, step-family transitions, and communication between extended family members.

→ Learn more about Divorce Mediation

Parenting Plans and Custody Arrangements

Creating a parenting plan is one of the most important tasks separating or divorced parents will face. We help you develop comprehensive, workable plans that cover physical custody schedules, legal decision-making authority, holiday and vacation arrangements, and communication protocols between households.

A well-crafted parenting plan puts your children's needs first — and gives both parents clarity about expectations.

→ Learn more about Child Custody Mediation

Co-Parenting Conflicts

Even with a solid parenting plan in place, co-parenting disagreements are common. Schedule changes, differences in parenting styles, new partners, relocation — these situations can create friction that affects the entire family.

Our co-parenting mediation sessions help you resolve current conflicts and develop better communication strategies for the future.

→ Learn more about Co-Parenting Mediation

Prenuptial and Postnuptial Agreements

Starting a marriage with transparent conversations about finances, property, and expectations isn't unromantic — it's wise. Mediation provides a collaborative, low-pressure environment to negotiate prenuptial or postnuptial agreements that protect both partners fairly.

Family Business Disputes

When family and business overlap, conflicts can become deeply personal. Whether you're dealing with disagreements about roles, compensation, succession planning, or the direction of the company, family business mediation helps you separate the personal from the professional and find workable solutions. Alisa's own experience running multiple businesses — including her umbrella company Synergy Relations — gives her firsthand insight into the dynamics of family-run enterprises.

Elder Care and Guardianship Decisions

As parents age, adult children often disagree about caregiving responsibilities, living arrangements, medical decisions, and financial management. These conversations are emotional and high-stakes. A skilled family mediator helps siblings and extended family members work through these decisions respectfully and reach consensus.

Sibling and Intergenerational Disputes

Inheritance expectations, perceived favoritism, estranged relationships, blended family dynamics — family conflict takes many forms. Mediation offers a structured opportunity to address grievances, clear the air, and find a path forward that preserves relationships whenever possible.

The Benefits of Family Mediation

Preserve Relationships

Litigation is adversarial by design. Mediation is collaborative. When you'll be connected to someone for life — as co-parents, siblings, or relatives — preserving the relationship matters as much as resolving the dispute.

Empower Every Voice

In mediation, everyone has the opportunity to share their perspective. There are no winners or losers — only solutions that reflect the needs of the whole family.

Save Time and Money

Family mediation resolves most disputes in a handful of sessions. Compared to court proceedings, the process is faster, less expensive, and far less emotionally draining.

Keep Matters Private

Family conflicts are personal. Mediation keeps your discussions confidential and out of the public record.

Create Lasting Solutions

Agreements that people help create are agreements they follow. Research shows that mediated agreements have significantly higher compliance rates than court-imposed orders.
