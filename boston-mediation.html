Our Boston Mediation Services

Divorce Mediation in Boston

Divorce is one of life’s most stressful transitions — but it doesn’t have to be a battle. Our divorce mediation process helps couples in the Boston area divide assets, establish support arrangements, and create parenting plans without the adversarial nature of courtroom proceedings.

Massachusetts is an equitable-distribution state, which means marital property is divided fairly — though not always equally — based on the circumstances of each case, and Massachusetts courts can consider a notably broad range of factors. Understanding how the court approaches property, debts, retirement accounts, and alimony requires careful analysis, and as an IRS Enrolled Agent (a credential Alisa earned in 2022, also applied through Compass Tax Center, our sister company specializing in tax services), she brings tax expertise most mediators simply cannot offer.

Understanding Massachusetts Court Procedures

If you’re considering divorce in the Boston area, your case will be filed in the Probate and Family Court for your county — Suffolk, Middlesex, Norfolk, or another. Massachusetts distinguishes between fault and no-fault divorce and has its own procedural requirements, from the nominal-fault waiting periods to detailed financial statements, which can feel overwhelming.

Compass Mediation Group helps you understand what the court requires so your mediated agreements meet all legal standards. Alisa’s 16 years of prior legal practice and courtroom experience mean she understands the procedural landscape from the inside. Although mediators do not provide legal advice to either party, our process is designed to ensure your agreements are thorough, informed, and ready for court approval.

Why Virtual Mediation Is Ideal for Boston

Between notorious traffic, limited parking, and New England winters, getting to an in-person appointment in the Boston area can be a real challenge. Virtual mediation eliminates that friction entirely.

Our clients tell us that participating from home actually improves the process. You’re in a familiar environment where you feel safe and grounded, there’s no anxiety about a shared waiting room, and parents don’t need to arrange extra childcare for travel time.

Every virtual session is conducted on a secure, confidential platform with virtual breakout rooms, so each party can have private, confidential conversations with the mediator during the session.
