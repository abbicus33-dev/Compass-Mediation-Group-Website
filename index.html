Why Choose Compass Mediation Group?

Compass Mediation Group has helped people navigate some of life's most challenging moments in divorce, family conflict, business disputes, and estate disagreements.

Founded and led by Alisa Kharis, every mediation session is guided by 25+ years of hands-on mediation experience, 16 years of litigation practice, and a genuine commitment to helping you move forward.

Mediation is not about winning or losing. It is about finding a path that respects everyone involved — and doing it in a way that saves you time, money, and emotional energy.
