Our Long Island & New York Mediation Services

Divorce Mediation in Lindenhurst & Long Island

Divorce is one of life’s most stressful transitions — but it doesn’t have to be a battle. Our divorce mediation process helps couples across Long Island and the New York area divide assets, establish support arrangements, and create parenting plans without the adversarial nature of courtroom proceedings.

New York is an equitable-distribution state, which means marital property is divided fairly — though not always equally — based on the circumstances of each case. Understanding how New York courts approach property, debts, retirement accounts, and maintenance requires careful analysis, and as an IRS Enrolled Agent (a credential Alisa earned in 2022, also applied through Compass Tax Center, our sister company specializing in tax services), she brings tax expertise most mediators simply cannot offer. Your settlement considers not just what’s fair today, but the long-term financial implications for both parties.

Family Mediation

Family disputes extend beyond divorce. Whether you need help with custody and parenting-plan modifications, elder-care decisions, or conflicts between family members, our family mediation services provide a neutral, structured environment where everyone’s voice is heard.

New York courts decide custody based on the best interests of the child. Drawing on her 16 years of family law and litigation experience, Alisa helps parents develop comprehensive plans that address custody and parenting-time schedules, decision-making authority, dispute-resolution methods, and relocation provisions.

Business and Estate Mediation

From Long Island’s small businesses and family enterprises to the wider New York market, partnership, contract, and succession disputes are an inevitable part of doing business. Our business and estate mediation services help stakeholders find workable solutions that preserve both relationships and assets. Alisa’s firsthand business-ownership experience — including founding and running Synergy Relations since 2003 — gives her practical insight into the realities business owners face during disputes.

Understanding New York Court Procedures

If you’re considering divorce on Long Island, your case will be filed in the Supreme Court for your county — Suffolk or Nassau — while New York City cases are filed in the Supreme Court for the relevant borough. New York’s trial-level court for divorce is confusingly named the Supreme Court, and its procedural requirements, from residency rules to detailed financial disclosure, can feel overwhelming.

Compass Mediation Group helps you understand what the court requires so your mediated agreements meet all legal standards. Alisa’s 16 years of prior legal practice and courtroom experience mean she understands the procedural landscape from the inside. Although mediators do not provide legal advice to either party, our process is designed to ensure your agreements are thorough, informed, and ready for court approval.

Why Virtual Mediation Works for Long Island & New York

Between Long Island Expressway traffic, the trip into the city, and the sheer sprawl of the metro area, getting to an in-person appointment can eat up an entire day. Virtual mediation eliminates that friction entirely.

Our clients tell us that participating from home actually improves the process. You’re in a familiar environment where you feel safe and grounded, there’s no anxiety about a shared waiting room, and parents don’t need to arrange extra childcare for travel time.

Every virtual session is conducted on a secure, confidential platform with virtual breakout rooms, so each party can have private, confidential conversations with the mediator during the session.
