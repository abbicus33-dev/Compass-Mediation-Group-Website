Privacy Policy

Overview

Compass Mediation Group is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of all parties involved in our mediation services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle your personal information in accordance with applicable privacy laws and professional mediation standards.

Information We Collect — Personal Information

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Names and contact information (address, phone number, email)
  • Employment and professional information
  • Financial information relevant to the dispute
  • Background information necessary for the mediation process
  • Communications and documentation related to your case

How We Collect Information

Information is collected through:

  • Initial intake forms and consultations
  • Direct communication with parties and their representatives
  • Documents provided by parties or their counsel
  • Court referrals or case management systems
  • Pre-mediation questionnaires

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information solely for:

  • Conducting mediation sessions and related services
  • Communicating with all parties about scheduling and process matters
  • Preparing mediation summaries (when requested and authorized)
  • Complying with legal and professional obligations
  • Billing and payment processing
  • Quality assurance and service improvement

Confidentiality and Mediation Privilege — Core Confidentiality Commitment

All information shared during mediation is treated as strictly confidential under:

  • State mediation confidentiality statutes
  • Professional mediation ethics rules
  • Our contractual confidentiality agreements

What This Means

  • Information disclosed during mediation cannot be used in subsequent legal proceedings
  • We do not disclose mediation communications to outside parties without explicit consent
  • Mediators cannot be compelled to testify about mediation content in most circumstances

Limited Exceptions

Confidentiality may be limited only when:

  • Required by law (such as mandatory reporting of child abuse)
  • All parties provide written consent to disclosure
  • Information relates to threats of violence or criminal activity
  • Court orders disclosure (subject to applicable privilege protections)

Information Sharing and Disclosure — Within the Mediation Process

We may share information:

  • Between parties during joint sessions (with consent)
  • With authorized representatives or attorneys
  • With interpreters or other necessary service providers (under confidentiality agreements)

External Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Limited disclosure may occur:

  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect the safety of individuals
  • With your explicit written consent
  • To our professional insurance carriers (under confidentiality protections)

Data Security and Protection — Security Measures

We implement appropriate safeguards including:

  • Secure file storage and document handling procedures
  • Encrypted digital communications when possible
  • Access controls and staff training on confidentiality
  • Secure disposal of documents when retention period expires

Data Retention

  • Active case files are maintained during the mediation process
  • Closed case materials are retained for 2 years, or as required by professional standards
  • Financial records are kept according to applicable accounting requirements
  • Documents are securely destroyed after retention periods expire

Your Rights and Choices — Access and Correction

You may:

  • Request access to your personal information in our files
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Ask questions about our privacy practices

Limitations

Some information may not be accessible if:

  • It contains confidential communications from other parties
  • Disclosure would violate mediation confidentiality rules
  • It consists of mediator work product or case notes

Third-Party Services — Service Providers

We may work with third-party providers for:

  • Video conferencing platforms
  • Document sharing services
  • Payment processing
  • Administrative support

All third-party providers are required to maintain confidentiality and handle information according to our privacy standards.

Communications — Electronic Communications

  • Email communications may not be fully secure
  • We use reasonable security measures for digital platforms
  • Parties are informed of any security limitations

Marketing Communications

We do not use mediation case information for marketing purposes without explicit consent.

Minors' Information

Special protections apply to information about minors:

  • Additional consent requirements may apply
  • Enhanced confidentiality protections
  • Compliance with child protection reporting requirements

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect:

  • Changes in applicable laws
  • Updates to our practices
  • Improvements in security measures

Parties will be notified of material changes through email.

International Considerations

If mediation involves parties in different jurisdictions:

  • We comply with applicable cross-border privacy requirements
  • Additional consent may be required for international information transfers
  • Parties are informed of any jurisdictional implications
