Our Phoenix Mediation Services

Divorce Mediation in Phoenix

Divorce is one of life’s most stressful transitions — but it doesn’t have to be a battle. Our divorce mediation process helps couples in the Phoenix area divide assets, establish support arrangements, and create parenting plans without the adversarial nature of courtroom proceedings.

Arizona is a community property state, which means assets and debts acquired during the marriage are generally considered jointly owned, subject to an equitable division. Navigating community property requires careful analysis, and as an IRS Enrolled Agent — a credential Alisa earned in 2022 and also applies through her work at Compass Tax Center, our sister company specializing in tax services — she brings tax expertise most mediators simply cannot offer. Your settlement considers not just what’s fair today, but the long-term financial implications for both parties.

Family Mediation and Parenting Plans

Arizona courts strongly encourage — and often require — mediation for custody and parenting-time disputes. Since 2013, Arizona has used the terms “legal decision-making” and “parenting time.” Our family mediation helps Phoenix parents build detailed parenting plans covering decision-making authority, schedules, holiday arrangements, and relocation provisions — all reflecting Arizona’s current legal framework.

With 16 years of family-law practice and courtroom experience, Alisa understands the level of detail Arizona courts expect — and helps with post-decree modifications when circumstances change.

Business and Estate Mediation

The Valley’s rapid growth has brought new businesses, franchises, and ventures across Phoenix and its suburbs. When partnerships fracture, contracts are disputed, or succession plans go sideways, our business and estate mediation services help stakeholders find solutions that protect the enterprise and preserve relationships. Alisa’s firsthand business-ownership experience — including founding and running Synergy Relations since 2003 — gives her practical insight into the realities business owners face. Estate mediation offers a dignified alternative to costly, public probate litigation.

Understanding Maricopa County Court Procedures

If you’re considering divorce in the Phoenix area, your case will be handled through Maricopa County Superior Court. Arizona’s Alternative Dispute Resolution programs reflect the state’s commitment to resolving disputes outside the courtroom, but court-connected sessions are typically brief and limited in scope.

Private mediation through Compass Mediation Group gives you the time and attention to address everything comprehensively — property division, support, and parenting plans — in a single, coordinated process. Arizona has a 60-day mandatory waiting period from the date of service before a divorce can be finalized; mediation can begin immediately, often letting you resolve everything before that period expires.

Why Virtual Mediation Is Ideal for Phoenix

Getting across the Valley can eat up an hour or more each way, and in summer, driving across Phoenix in 110°+ heat for a meeting that could happen online is simply unnecessary. Virtual mediation eliminates that friction entirely.

Our clients tell us that participating from home actually improves the process. You’re in a familiar environment where you feel safe and grounded, there’s no anxiety about a shared waiting room, and parents don’t need to arrange extra childcare for travel time.

Every virtual session is conducted on a secure, confidential platform with virtual breakout rooms, so each party can have private, confidential conversations with the mediator — just as in separate rooms during an in-person mediation.
