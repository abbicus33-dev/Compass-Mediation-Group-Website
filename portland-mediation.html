Our Portland Mediation Services

Divorce Mediation in Portland

Divorce is one of life’s most stressful transitions — but it doesn’t have to be a battle. Our divorce mediation process helps couples in the Portland area divide assets, establish support arrangements, and create parenting plans without the adversarial nature of courtroom proceedings.

Oregon is an equitable-distribution state, which means marital property is divided fairly — though not always equally — based on the circumstances of each case. Understanding how Oregon courts approach property, debts, retirement accounts, and support requires careful analysis, and as an IRS Enrolled Agent — a credential Alisa earned in 2022 and also applies through her work at Compass Tax Center, our sister company specializing in tax services — she brings tax expertise most mediators simply cannot offer. Your settlement considers not just what’s fair today, but the long-term financial implications for both parties.

Family Mediation

Family disputes extend beyond divorce. Whether you need help with parenting-plan modifications, elder-care decisions, or conflicts between family members, our family mediation services provide a neutral, structured environment where everyone’s voice is heard.

Oregon requires a parenting plan in any case involving children. Drawing on her 16 years of family law, civil litigation, and general practice experience, Alisa helps parents develop comprehensive plans that address parenting-time schedules, decision-making authority, dispute-resolution methods, and relocation provisions — all centered on the best interests of your children.

Business and Estate Mediation

Partnership disagreements, succession-planning conflicts, and estate disputes can fracture relationships and destroy value. Portland’s diverse economy — from downtown professional services to a thriving small-business and maker community — generates its share of commercial disputes that benefit from mediation over litigation. Our business and estate mediation services help stakeholders find workable solutions that preserve both relationships and assets. Alisa’s extensive business background — including founding and running Synergy Relations, a consultation and strategic communications firm, since 2003 — gives her practical insight into the realities business owners face during disputes.

Understanding Multnomah County Court Procedures

If you’re considering divorce in the Portland area, your case will be filed in Multnomah County Circuit Court (or the circuit court for your county). While Oregon courts encourage mediation — and require it in many custody and parenting-time disputes — navigating the procedural requirements, from the 90-day waiting period to financial disclosure obligations, can feel overwhelming.

Compass Mediation Group helps you understand what the court requires so your mediated agreements meet all legal standards. Alisa’s 16 years of prior legal practice, including extensive trial and motions experience, means she understands the procedural landscape from the inside. Although mediators do not provide legal advice to either party, our process is designed to ensure your agreements are thorough, informed, and ready for court approval.

Why Virtual Mediation Works for Portland

Portland’s geography — split by rivers, bridges, and sprawling suburbs — can make in-person meetings a logistical headache. Bridge lifts, I-5 and I-205 congestion, and the crossing to Vancouver can turn a short trip into an ordeal. Virtual mediation eliminates that friction entirely.

Our clients tell us that participating from home actually improves the process. You’re in a familiar environment where you feel safe and grounded. There’s no anxiety about sitting in a waiting room. And for families with children, there’s no need to arrange extended childcare for travel time.

Every virtual session is conducted on a secure, confidential platform with virtual breakout rooms, so each party can think, regroup, or consult privately during the session — with the same professionalism and structure as an in-person meeting.
