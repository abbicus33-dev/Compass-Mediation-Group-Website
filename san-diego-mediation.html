Our San Diego Mediation Services

Divorce Mediation in San Diego

Divorce is one of life’s most stressful transitions — but it doesn’t have to be a battle. Our divorce mediation process helps couples in the San Diego area divide assets, establish support arrangements, and create parenting plans without the adversarial nature of courtroom proceedings.

California is a community property state, which means assets and debts acquired during the marriage are generally divided equally. Navigating community property — especially with real estate, military benefits, and retirement accounts — requires careful analysis, and as an IRS Enrolled Agent (a credential Alisa earned in 2022, also applied through Compass Tax Center, our sister company specializing in tax services), she brings tax expertise most mediators simply cannot offer. Your settlement considers not just what’s fair today, but the long-term financial implications for both parties.

Military Divorce Mediation

San Diego is home to one of the nation’s largest military communities, and many local families face the unique complexities of military divorce. Division of military pensions under the Uniformed Services Former Spouses’ Protection Act (USFSPA), Survivor Benefit Plan elections, and TRICARE transitions all require a mediator with both legal depth and financial acumen. Alisa’s background as a former practicing attorney and IRS Enrolled Agent makes her well-suited to guide military families through these layered issues.

Family Mediation

Family disputes extend beyond divorce. Whether you need help with custody and parenting-plan modifications, elder-care decisions, or conflicts between family members, our family mediation services provide a neutral, structured environment where everyone’s voice is heard. Drawing on her 16 years of family law and litigation experience, Alisa helps parents develop comprehensive plans centered on the best interests of your children.

Business and Estate Mediation

San Diego’s economy — from biotech and defense to tourism and a thriving small-business community — generates its share of partnership, contract, and succession disputes that benefit from mediation over litigation. Our business and estate mediation services help stakeholders find workable solutions that preserve both relationships and assets. Alisa’s firsthand business-ownership experience — including founding and running Synergy Relations since 2003 — gives her practical insight into the realities business owners face.

Understanding San Diego County Court Procedures

If you’re considering divorce in the San Diego area, your case will be filed in San Diego County Superior Court. While California courts encourage mediation, navigating the procedural requirements, from the six-month waiting period to detailed financial disclosure obligations, can feel overwhelming.

Compass Mediation Group helps you understand what the court requires so your mediated agreements meet all legal standards. Alisa’s 16 years of prior legal practice and courtroom experience mean she understands the procedural landscape from the inside. Although mediators do not provide legal advice to either party, our process is designed to ensure your agreements are thorough, informed, and ready for court approval.

Why Virtual Mediation Is Ideal for San Diego

From the coast to East County, San Diego’s sprawl and freeway congestion can turn a simple appointment into a half-day affair. Virtual mediation eliminates that friction entirely.

Our clients tell us that participating from home actually improves the process. You’re in a familiar environment where you feel safe and grounded, there’s no anxiety about a shared waiting room, and for military families managing demanding schedules, there’s no need to juggle duty obligations around travel time.

Every virtual session is conducted on a secure, confidential platform with virtual breakout rooms, so each party can have private, confidential conversations with the mediator during the session.
