Credentials

Credentials & Qualifications

Alisa brings an exceptional combination of credentials that set her apart in the mediation field:

Master Mediator — International Mediation Association (IMA), 2021

Alisa holds the Master Mediator designation from the International Mediation Association (IMA), earned through a rigorous year-long training program in Phoenix, AZ. This is the highest level of professional certification available in the mediation field, reflecting advanced training in conflict resolution, negotiation strategy, facilitation techniques, and ethical standards — as well as extensive practical experience mediating complex disputes.

It is worth noting that the mediation field is largely unregulated — there are no formal educational requirements to practice as a mediator beyond basic continuing education in most jurisdictions, and virtually anyone can call themselves a mediator. This makes voluntary, rigorous credentialing like the IMA Master Mediator designation especially meaningful. Alisa pursued this certification not because it was required, but because she believes her clients deserve the highest standard of professional training and accountability.

Juris Doctorate — Seattle University School of Law (1995)

Alisa earned her law degree from Seattle University School of Law in 1995, one of the Pacific Northwest's most respected legal institutions. Her legal education provides the foundation for understanding the legal frameworks that surround divorce, custody, property division, and business disputes — ensuring that mediated agreements are not only fair but also legally sound.

B.A. Political Science, Cum Laude — Washington State University (1987)

Alisa graduated Cum Laude from Washington State University with a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science, building the analytical, research, and critical thinking skills that have underpinned her entire professional career.

IRS Enrolled Agent (2022, with Oregon License)

Alisa is a federally licensed IRS Enrolled Agent, authorized to represent taxpayers before the Internal Revenue Service. She earned this credential in 2022 and also holds an Oregon license. This credential is particularly valuable in divorce and business mediation, where tax implications can significantly affect financial settlements. Understanding how asset division, spousal support, and retirement account distribution are taxed allows Alisa to help clients make fully informed financial decisions. She also applies her Enrolled Agent expertise at H&R Block, where she has served since 2021 — keeping her tax knowledge sharp and current.

40-Hour Mediation Training — Aberdeen, WA (2016 & 2020)

Alisa completed comprehensive 40-hour mediation training programs in Aberdeen, WA in both 2016 and 2020, supplementing her IMA training and decades of practical experience with structured education in mediation methodology, techniques, and best practices.
